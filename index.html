About This Site

Start with familiar mathematics and follow it toward ideas used in PhD qualifying exams. Each current topic has high-school, undergraduate, master's, and PhD-level sections. These names describe mathematical depth; every section explains its notation and points out the background it uses.

Tap or keyboard-open the explanation beside a symbol to hear the whole expression in plain language, see what its symbols mean here, and work through an example. The site currently covers four foundation topics; advanced modules are planned.
